Hi, I’m Briana!

I am currently a pre-med student at the University of Virginia with a passion for helping middle and high school students become more confident in challenging academic subjects.

My academic background includes advanced coursework across mathematics, science, and English, including AP Calculus BC, AP Statistics, AP Biology, AP Chemistry, AP Literature, and AP Language.

Over the past two years, I have worked with middle and high school students through one-on-one tutoring and structured peer-learning environments.

My tutoring style emphasizes patience, clarity, and breaking complicated material into manageable steps. Rather than simply giving students answers, I help them understand the reasoning behind what they are doing so they can become more independent and confident learners.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are sessions online?

Yes. All tutoring sessions are webcam-based, making scheduling easier and allowing students to learn comfortably from home.

What ages do you tutor?

I primarily work with middle and high school students.

Can you help with homework?

Absolutely. Homework can be incorporated into tutoring sessions, but I focus on teaching students how to solve problems independently rather than simply completing assignments for them.

Do you help with essays?

Yes. I can help students brainstorm, organize, revise, and strengthen essays while preserving the student's own ideas and writing voice.

How much does tutoring cost?

Standard academic tutoring is $35 per hour. SAT Reading & Writing tutoring is $45 per hour.

How does payment work?

Payment is accepted through Zelle or Venmo after the tutoring schedule has been confirmed.

Do you offer an introductory session?

Yes. New students can schedule a short introductory meeting to discuss their goals, current coursework, and what they would like help with.
