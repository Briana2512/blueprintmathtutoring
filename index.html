Hi, I'm Briana!

UVA Pre-Med Student 4 Years of Tutoring Experience Personalized 1-on-1 Support

I'm a pre-med student at the University of Virginia, and I know how frustrating it can feel when a subject just isn't clicking. That's why I love helping students turn those moments of confusion into understanding and confidence.

My academic background spans mathematics, science, and English, including advanced coursework in AP Calculus BC, AP Statistics, AP Biology, AP Chemistry, AP Literature, and AP Language. I bring that foundation into every session, adapting my explanations to the way each student learns best.

Over the past four years, I've worked with middle and high school students through one-on-one tutoring and collaborative learning. I've learned that sometimes all it takes is a different explanation, a little patience, or the confidence to ask one more question.

My goal is simple: to help students understand the why behind what they're learning, not just memorize the steps to get an answer. Whether we're working through an algebra problem, preparing for an exam, or strengthening an essay, I want students to leave each session feeling more capable than when they started.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are sessions online?

Yes. All tutoring sessions are webcam-based, making scheduling easier and allowing students to learn comfortably from home.

What ages do you tutor?

I primarily work with middle and high school students.

Can you help with homework?

Absolutely. Homework can be incorporated into tutoring sessions, but I focus on teaching students how to solve problems independently rather than simply completing assignments for them.

Do you help with essays?

Yes. I can help students brainstorm, organize, revise, and strengthen essays while preserving the student's own ideas and writing voice.

How much does tutoring cost?

Standard academic tutoring is $40 per hour. SAT Math and SAT Reading & Writing tutoring is $45 per hour.

How does payment work?

Payment is accepted through Zelle or Venmo after the tutoring schedule has been confirmed.

Do you offer an introductory session?

Yes! You can schedule a free 30-minute consultation to discuss your goals, current coursework, and what you would like help with.

How can I leave a review?

Click the Leave a Review button above and complete the review form. Reviews are automatically displayed on the website.
